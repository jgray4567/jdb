JDB Entertainment Global proudly presents Songster Bus™ USA Goodwill Music Tour
Coming in the summer of 2027!

An international music platform where songs, artists, and storytelling come together to unite nations through shared creativity.

At its core, Songster Bus™ is about creating music with meaning—building bridges, strengthening relationships, and proving that music remains a powerful force for global connection. With world-class talent and industry expertise, the tour produces original music designed for international reach and lasting impact.

This unprecedented initiative blends cultural exchange with global goodwill, pairing talented artists from around the world with Grammy and award-winning mentors to create original music that reflects the spirit of collaboration across cultures.

ANTHEM Foundation

A non-profit organization, in cooperation with JDB Entertainment Global, the ANTHEM Foundation supports cultural exchanges in the United States and among countries of the world to foster good will, create greater awareness and better understanding among people of different cultures and backgrounds, and to uplift and unify the human spirit.

JDB Entertainment Global Legacy

The Songster Bus USA Goodwill Music Tour is a national and international goodwill music platform powered by purpose, story, and global connection. At its core it is more than a tour, it’s a new movement. The Songster Bus USA Goodwill Music Tour uses original music, artist development, and storytelling to connect cultures, elevate emerging talent, and create long-term international partnerships, while delivering high value to brands aligned with creativity, culture, and purpose. Building global cooperation, culture to culture, artist to artist, country to country, year after year.
