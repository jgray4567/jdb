DAVE BROOKS

Celebrity Master Vocal Coach
Executive Producer JDB Entertainment Global
Songster Bus™ USA Goodwill Music Tour

Mr. Brooks is a master and celebrity vocal coach of the stars and top producer. He has been involved in the music industry for over 25 years acting as a premier session singer, songwriter, songwriting coach, vocal coach, and expert vocal communicator.

Dave travels nationally and internationally featuring a series of artist intensive master classes. He has traveled to Japan with a highly acclaimed intensive master class program. His voice has been featured on national network campaigns for ABC, NBC, CBS, “The Tonight Show”, “Wheel of Fortune”, and a host of top commercial advertisements and national campaigns.

Dave’s ability to sing and interpret many different styles of music has made him one of the world’s most highly sought after vocal coaches and artistic directors. He is the co- author of the web’s number one and best-selling vocal instruction course in the world, “Singing Success”. He was the author of the world-class, online video, tutorial series, “The Virtual Vocal Teacher”, a catalog of systematic, vocal tutorials.

During his career, Dave was awarded two production deals with Dreamworks and Warner Music. A few of the many industry-leaders and performing artists Dave has worked with are: Warner Music Group, Sony Music, Icon Studios – Atlanta, Taylor Swift, Toby Keith, Keith Follese (ASCAP’s songwriter of the year), Neil Thrasher (ASCAP’s songwriter of the year), Keith Thomas (Grammy award winning writer and producer), Brett Eldredge, Crystal Nicole, Canaan Smith, Chase Rice, Frankie Ballard, Big Smo, Pam Tillis, Rascal Flatts, Michael Ray, Kix Brooks of ''Brooks and Dunn", Gladys Knight, James Ingram, Mark Kibble of “Take 6”, Hayley Williams of the group Paramore, Debbie Allen - Golden Globe and Emmy award-winning actress and producer, and Tim Davis, musical director for the hit TV series, “Glee”. Dave is also an “American Idol” prep vocal coach.

Dave has also teamed up with media veteran Mrs. Joan Betros to form JDB Entertainment Global, a new power house live event and TV media company with headquarters in the USA. Along with Joan, he is the co-creator of the Forte Music Con™ an educational cultural exchange and music industry event being introduced to the Kingdom of SaudiArabia. He is also co-producer of “The Nashville Connection – Heroes Salute”,a 4-day music industry experience that educates and inspires young artists.

In 2017, Dave was invited by Mrs. Betros and sponsored by Almontage Entertainment KSA to travel to Saudi Arabia where he successfully instructed a gratis two-day workshop, the “Shine Master Classes”.He instructed music industry vocalists and created a songwriting seminar for aspiring and up and coming Saudi artists and entrepreneurs. He is looking forward to returning to the Kingdom with JDB Entertainment Global to produce and participate in the Forte Music Con™ in 2024.
